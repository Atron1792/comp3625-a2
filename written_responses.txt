Question 3:
In this assignment scenario, which party is acting according to the principle of maximum expected utililty? The customer? Your program? Both?

Both the customer and the program are acting according to the principle of maximum expected utility. The customer is trying to maximize their
utility by purchasing insurance such that the expected utility lost by purchasing the insurance is not greater than the expected utlity lost
by going uninsured and being forced to pay out of pocket. The program is trying to maximize its expected utility by offering an insurance premium
that allows them to make a profit, at minimum, while not charging so much that a customer does not accept the premium.
